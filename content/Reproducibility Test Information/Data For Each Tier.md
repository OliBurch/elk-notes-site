##### Real datasets (all tiers):
Outcome measures:
- **R1** — Winter aerial survey counts of elk, 1972–2005
- **R2** — Summer counts of elk remaining on the winter range
- **R3** — Radiocollar and GPS telemetry, early (1977–80) and late (2002–04) periods
Candidate covariates:
- **R4** — Total June–August precipitation
- **R5** — Winter severity index (North Pacific Oscillation)
- **R6** — Wolf abundance index
- **R7** — Number of elk relocated
- **R8** — Total elk harvest
- **R9** — Winter horse numbers
- **R10** — Cumulative area burned
- **R11** — Cumulative winter range enhancement area
##### Decoys — Group A: non-biological (12):
No living organism involved. Clean, but easy to dismiss in a single sweep, which is why they cannot be the whole list.

|     | Dataset                                                       | Why the pathway is severed                                                                                                                                                   |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D1  | Archaeological and cultural site inventories                  | Records of past occupation; no temporal signal across the study period, no contemporary effect on forage, predation or human presence                                        |
| D2  | Snow-course station **maintenance logs**                      | Servicing and calibration records only — contains no snow measurements, so carries no winter severity signal                                                                 |
| D3  | Groundwater well-level monitoring                             | Subsurface aquifer levels; no link to surface forage phenology or to predator or human distribution                                                                          |
| D4  | Seismic monitoring station records                            | Instrumentation for earth movement; no ecological pathway of any kind                                                                                                        |
| D5  | Aerial photograph **acquisition metadata**                    | Flight dates and coverage extents only — no imagery, therefore no vegetation or land-cover signal                                                                            |
| D6  | Cadastral survey benchmark records                            | Positions of land survey monuments; administrative and static                                                                                                                |
| D7  | Telecommunications repeater site inventories                  | Locations of installed infrastructure; not a proxy for human activity levels, which are recorded separately                                                                  |
| D8  | Paleontological site records                                  | Fossil localities; no contemporary process                                                                                                                                   |
| D9  | Water well drilling logs                                      | Subsurface geology and well construction records                                                                                                                             |
| D10 | Provincial land title and parcel transfer records             | Administrative ownership changes; no physical or ecological change implied                                                                                                   |
| D11 | Geodetic GPS reference station records                        | Fixed survey control points; positional reference data only                                                                                                                  |
| D12 | Weather station **instrument calibration and servicing logs** | Servicing records, not meteorological measurements. Deliberately tempting — precipitation and winter severity are both real covariates, so the word "weather" invites a grab |
##### Decoys — Group B: biological, broken pathway (6):
Alive, plausibly "wildlife data", genuinely inert to ungulate movement. Each needs its severing argument written down because a reviewer will test them.

|     | Dataset                                   | Why the pathway is severed                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D13 | Bat acoustic monitoring                   | Insectivorous; not forage competitors with elk, not prey for elk predators, no shared resource                                                                                                                                                          |
| D14 | Butterfly and pollinator transect surveys | Route to elk forage would run through plant reproduction on a timescale far longer than a migration decision — and the key forage species here (rough fescue and other grasses) are wind-pollinated                                                     |
| D15 | Moth light-trap catch records             | Nocturnal insects; bat prey, no ungulate relevance                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| D16 | Ground beetle pitfall trapping            | Soil-surface predatory invertebrates; no trophic or habitat link to ungulates                                                                                                                                                                           |
| D17 | Songbird point counts                     | No direct pathway, **but** songbird communities track forest succession, so a model may reasonably offer them as a habitat-change proxy. Redundant rather than wrong — burn area and enhancement area are both supplied directly. See open ruling below |
| D18 | Amphibian breeding-pond surveys           | Wetland-breeding amphibians. **Thinnest severing argument in the set** — a model could claim amphibians as prey subsidising predators. Retained but flagged; first candidate for cutting if it generates scoring disputes                               |
