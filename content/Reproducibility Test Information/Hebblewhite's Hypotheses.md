##### The eight source hypotheses:

|        | Hypothesis               | Mechanism in brief                                                                                                | Dataset offered      | Outcome                        |
| ------ | ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **H1** | Elk harvest              | Hunting removes residents preferentially, since migrants are away for much of the season — should raise the ratio | Elk harvest          | **Failed** — observed opposite |
| **H2** | Elk relocations          | Capture bias toward migrants, or disruption of learned migratory behaviour                                        | Elk relocated        | Consistent; not ruled out      |
| **H3** | Prescribed fire          | Burning improves summer-range forage, favouring migrants                                                          | Cumulative burn area | **Failed** on the ratio        |
| **H4** | Winter range enhancement | Improved winter range is good year-round, so residents gain most                                                  | Enhancement area     | **Consistent**                 |
| **H5** | Winter horse numbers     | Fewer horses means less grazing competition, benefiting residents                                                 | Horse numbers        | **Failed** on population       |
| **H6** | Hay feeding              | Reliable artificial food plus habituation to humans erodes the reason to leave                                    | *(none — held out)*  | **Consistent**                 |
| **H7** | Wolf predation           | Migration buys spatial separation from wolves, so migrants should do better                                       | Wolf index           | **Failed** — observed opposite |
| **H8** | Provincial wolf harvest  | Wolves protected inside the park but hunted outside reverses that advantage                                       | Wolf index           | **Consistent**                 |
